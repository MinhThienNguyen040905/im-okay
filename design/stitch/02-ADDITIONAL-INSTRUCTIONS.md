# Additional instructions cho Stitch Web

## Nội dung cần dán

Design a product named “I’m Okay”, a safety check-in experience for people who live alone.

The product has two connected surfaces inside the same Stitch project:

1. A native mobile app for the person being protected.
2. A mobile-first responsive web experience opened by trusted contacts from email links.

Use the uploaded I’m Okay DESIGN.md as the single source of truth for colors, typography, spacing, shapes and components. Do not invent a second theme. Keep all future screens visually consistent with the approved anchor screens.

All visible product copy must be natural Vietnamese with correct accents. The tone must be calm, warm, trustworthy and non-judgmental. Do not use frightening, clinical or surveillance language. Never claim that I’m Okay is a medical device, rescue service or guaranteed life-saving system.

Mobile reference viewport: 390 × 844 px. Web must be mobile-first at 390 px and adapt cleanly to 768 px and 1440 px without becoming a wide dashboard.

Product rules:

- Check-in must be the only dominant action on the mobile Home screen.
- Warning, active alert, SOS and drill must be clearly distinguishable by icon, wording and state, not color alone.
- SOS must require a three-second hold or an accessible two-step alternative.
- Snooze must always have an automatic end time.
- Trusted contacts do not need an account to respond on the web.
- Do not expose location, health data, private tokens or the full contact list on public links.
- Do not add SMS, voice calling, continuous location, AI diagnosis, payment or emergency-service integration to the MVP UI.
- Use large touch targets, clear focus states and layouts that tolerate longer Vietnamese text and accessibility font scaling.

Use these consistent sample data across screens:

- Protected user: Minh Anh.
- Trusted contacts: Lan, Tuấn and Mẹ.
- Last check-in: 20:15, 01/08/2026.
- Next deadline: 08:15, 03/08/2026.
- Check-in interval: 36 hours.
- Sample contact email: lan.nguyen@example.com.

Create only the screen requested in each future prompt. Preserve previously approved components and navigation patterns unless the prompt explicitly asks for a targeted change.
